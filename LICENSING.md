This guide explains how Super Durable applies "internal business purposes" in
the Sustainable Use License 1.0. It does not modify or expand the license.

Previously released versions remain available under the terms that accompanied
those releases.

## Internal business purposes

You may use Superverse for your own internal business operations, regardless of your
company's revenue or whether your company competes with Super Durable.

You may also use Superverse as hidden backend infrastructure for a commercial product
when the product's primary value comes from other functionality and customers
do not receive direct access to a substantial set of Superverse features.

Without a commercial license, you may not white-label, host, or resell Superverse, or
build an automation platform, process platform, workflow platform, or
software-building platform whose value derives entirely or substantially from
Superverse.

## Distribution

You may distribute Superverse or modifications only free of charge for non-commercial
purposes. Recipients must receive the applicable license and notices, and
modified copies must identify that they were modified.

## Consulting and support

You may charge for consulting, installation, workflow development, or support
services. Those services must not provide customers with hosted access to a
substantial set of Superverse features.

## Common scenarios

| Scenario | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Run Superverse inside your company to build internal applications | Allowed |
| Use Superverse as a hidden backend for a distinct product feature | Allowed |
| Build a platform whose value derives entirely or substantially from Superverse | Commercial license required |
| Host Superverse as a service for others, white-label, or resell it | Commercial license required |
| Provide consulting or support without hosted Superverse access | Allowed |
| Redistribute Superverse free of charge for a non-commercial purpose | Allowed |
| Sell or commercially redistribute Superverse or a modified version | Commercial license required |
