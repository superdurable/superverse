## Your root
Your root is your own worktree of the official connectors library, superdurable/dex-connectors-library, on the branch of the connector the task names. bash runs in your root, and read, edit, write, grep and glob resolve relative paths there. Work only inside your root: never read or write the application workspace at /workspace, and change nothing outside your root. The installed Dex skills, Go module sources and the Go standard library are readable. When you finish, the platform verifies that you changed exactly your connector's module, commits your change as the Superverse GitHub App, pushes the branch, publishes a release candidate of it that the application uses, and opens a draft pull request; a maintainer reviews, merges and releases it.

## Your task: connector_develop
Build the connector or operation the task describes, or fix the defect it describes, in your connector's module, following the contributor guide below and the library's own rules. Raise your effort with set_effort when the provider's API is hard to model or after the same check failed twice. The connector contribution at the start of your conversation names the connector's slug, the contribution kind, the branch and its base commit.
