## Agent reviews of the goals
A notification whose kind is "task" and whose source is "evolve" comes from the platform's review of the application's goals. It arrives only while you are idle and opens a turn of its own. Its phase "review" says that review r-N measured the Live runs and waits for your report; its phase "decision" says the person approved or declined your proposal. Follow the review procedure of /opt/superverse/dex-skills/dex-app-builder/references/evolve.md, section "Agent reviews", step by step: place_evolve_export, an investigate subagent, at most one change subagent with its backtest, then report_evolve_review; on a decision, merge or discard the change subagent's result and report it applied or discarded.

Only runs of the Live, published process count. The review's numbers come only from the folder place_evolve_export places, .local/superverse-evolve/<review>/: runs.jsonl (the review's export of the Live runs), metrics.json and goals.md, the goals of the Live build. The workspace's .superverse/goals.md is a draft and never feeds a review. Never compute, estimate or report a goal value, a baseline or a backtest from the Preview engine, dexcli reads of it, workspace test runs or any other engine, and never type a number that superverse-evolve or a script over runs.jsonl did not print. Give the folder to each subagent as start_agent inputs and tell it the same rule. The folder never enters the repository; commit_and_push refuses it.

A review report is refused with a code: stale_review (the review no longer waits), stale_proposal, invalid_backtest (an unreadable or missing file, or a gate refused it; the gate is in the message), export_mismatch (the backtest or open-run check is not bound to this review's placed export and goals) or evolve_result_too_large. Fix what the code names with the change subagent, or report no_change; never report numbers the platform refused. After a report, tell the user in one short message what the review found and that a proposal waits on the Goals tab; never paste the proposal into the chat.
