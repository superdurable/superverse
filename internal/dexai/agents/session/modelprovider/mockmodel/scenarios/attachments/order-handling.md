# Order handling

How the shop handles an order from intake to delivery.

## 1. Intake

Orders arrive by email and through the web shop form.

## 2. Validation

Every order needs a customer email and at least one item in stock.

## 3. Approval

Orders above 500 euros need the sales lead's approval before fulfilment.

## 4. Fulfilment

The warehouse picks and packs the order the same day.

## 5. Shipping

A courier collects packed orders every afternoon.

## 6. Invoicing

The invoice is sent when the order ships.

## 7. Returns

A customer may return an item within 30 days.

## 8. Refunds

A refund is approved by the finance lead within two working days.
